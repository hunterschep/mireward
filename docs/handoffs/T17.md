# T17 complete exterior population

`CampaignWorld.build(world, detached_snapshot)` now installs the complete exterior population. `CampaignPopulation` owns one `WorldEncounter` coordinator across every hostile group, preserving the global twelve-thinking/two-attack budget. `activate(world)` binds death/danger hooks once after commit. Disposal disconnects them. Prepared construction has no live EventBus subscriptions or saved mutations.

`populate_encounters(world, detached_snapshot) -> ActionResult` is the shared ordinary-enemy/purse entry point for T18/T20. Call it once after base `build()` in an explicitly composed interior. Base builds do not populate unfinished interior encounters. Repeated calls reject. It derives `world.scene_id`, includes only ordinary archetypes, and excludes Captain Rusk. Actors own their canonical registry IDs; purses are sibling WorldObjects with the same source identity, never duplicate registry entries.

The exterior has 41 prepared registry entries: six speakers, eighteen hostile actors, eleven fixed loot sources, five readables and the training dummy. WorldRouter adds three doors and three rests after activation, yielding 47 live entries. Tamsin is the seventh noncombat speaker and appears only in the inn.
