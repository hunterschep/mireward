## Encounter behavior

Rusk starts neutral, invulnerable and available to the existing DialogueService. Leave does not start the fight. The real Challenge choice routes through GameUI to challenge; successful acceptance closes the gate, enables the hostile hurtbox and participates in the shared coordinator's fresh danger check. The HUD's existing `is_duel_active` hook shows the boss bar only during the fight.

At 220 HP and 20% armor, he alternates:

| Pattern | Raw damage | Reach | Windup / active / recovery |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Thrust | 24 | 2.5 m | 0.75 / 0.15 / 0.85 s |
| Sweep | 22 | 2.2 m | 0.90 / 0.20 / 1.10 s |

Both use shared swept single-victim damage and can be parried. At or below 110 HP, the canonical “Very well. No more ceremony.” line and phase flag occur once. Current attack_profile and phase_elapsed are untouched. Future recovery becomes 85% of its original duration; windup/active/damage remain unchanged. The idle stance changes after committed animation finishes. After each pair of attacks, including parried attempts once their stagger finishes, Rusk has an additional 0.85-second breath with his blade lowered. He remains vulnerable.

Death immediately ends the duel and opens the gate. Player death opens it without awarding victory; actual recovery resets living Rusk through the existing EnemyActor reset hook. Loading creates a full-health neutral living boss with an open gate; a saved defeated boss remains dead. No boss HP, phase or gate-closed flag is persisted. Temporary tree removal disconnects live listeners without deliberately resetting committed phases, allowing the router's existing rollback activation path to restore the scene.

Root reconciled the save/load contract in `9e706e3`: saves remain forbidden in danger, while an explicit Load may restore a live encounter when the player has no committed attack/guard/healing/recovery/transaction/travel. The boss module does not change SaveService policy.
