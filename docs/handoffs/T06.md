## Behavior delivered

CombatComponent provides WINDUP → ACTIVE → RECOVERY → IDLE for all four equipped swords and their heavy attacks. Cost commits once; the current weapon profile remains captured for that attack. One light follow-up can be buffered during the last 0.12 seconds of light recovery. Guard/healing/equipment cannot cancel a committed attack. Menu input clearing removes buffered input, preserving the phase, cost, damage record, and cooldown.

Spatial contact uses sampled capsule volumes across both weapon arc and actor/camera movement. Samples are at most 5 degrees or 0.10 meters apart, including rapid camera rotation. The blade endpoint and nearest hurtbox surface are bounded by configured reach. World rays to contact and actor hurtbox center reject walls, including protruding hurtboxes. Each swing consumes at most one eligible nearest contact. Self, same-faction, neutral, dead, despawning, duplicate, and malformed requests are rejected. Player/hostile attacks use the same path.

CombatMath remains the sole guard/armor calculation. Incoming raw damage is already multiplied once. Player defense implements horizontal ±60-degree guard, 0.18-second parry, five-stamina cost, 0.65-second opening cooldown, 0.9-second guard break, and 0.20-second health-loss immunity that rejected hits cannot extend. Actual swept parries stagger the attacking component. Death emits one stable-ID signal. This component grants no persistent defeats, currency, or loot.

### APIs for successors

- `configure_player(player: MirePlayer, stable_id: StringName = &"player") -> void`.
- `configure_enemy(actor: Node3D, stable_id: StringName, definition: MireTypes.EnemyDef, faction: StringName = &"hostile") -> void`.
- `request_attack(kind: StringName) -> MireTypes.ActionResult`, `set_guard(held: bool) -> void`, `receive_hit(request: MireTypes.DamageRequest) -> MireTypes.DamageResult`.
- `apply_stagger(seconds: float)`, `reset_combat()`, `clear_input_edges()`, `advance(delta: float)`, `is_committed()`, `get_health()`, `get_stamina()`.
- `set_attack_profile(kind, profile) -> ActionResult` accepts positive finite `damage`, `windup`, `active`, `recovery`, `reach`; optional nonnegative `stamina` and `arc_degrees` in 0–120. Profile replacement during an attack fails. Boss thrust/sweep use this method and the same damage path.
- Readable runtime fields: `phase`, `phase_elapsed`, `attack_kind`, `attack_sequence`, `attack_profile`, `guard_held`, `dead`, `stagger_remaining`, `immunity_remaining`.
- Enemy-only writable transient resources: `health`, `max_health`, `guard_stamina`, `armor`, `shield_multiplier`. Player getters read GameSession's snapshot instead.
- Signals: `phase_changed(phase)`, `hit_received(request, result)`, `hit_landed(victim_id, result)`, `died(entity_id)`, `staggered(seconds)`, `feedback(outcome)`.
- `CombatHurtbox.configure(component, radius=0.32, height=1.6)` is a one-time construction operation. Parent it to the actor at Y=0.9. It is not a physics body and has collision mask zero.

PlayerVitals is the sole player stamina spend/delay/regeneration owner. Combat never runs a second player regeneration loop. The enemy component owns 40 guard stamina and 10/s recovery outside guarding; AI must not regenerate that resource again. Combat physics priority is -20, ahead of later healing completion handlers; T09 should consume `hit_received` before committing a same-tick heal. External action locks retain pose ownership, allowing healing presentation.

Reset combat only during deliberate load/new-game/recovery/travel reconstruction after resources are installed. Do not reset it on modal close. Outgoing sequence numbers increase globally during the process lifetime, so re-created actors cannot reuse an old attack number. The per-victim received ledger is bounded by attacker IDs and clears on deliberate reset.
