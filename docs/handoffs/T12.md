## Behavior delivered

All six main quests and all six side quests have explicit objective IDs, giver/turn-in identities, immutable predicates, physical targets, scene/location hints, exact rewards, consumption and durable effects. Main unlocks are sequential; side quests are available independently and stay out of the journal until accepted. Early item, readable, puzzle and defeat evidence reconciles when a quest activates or the session loads. READY means the final return/confirmation is available; completing records that final step in the same transaction as the reward.

Completion stages consumed keys, retained evidence, crowns, item deliveries, pending overflow, quest records, successor unlocks and story effects together. Wren terms commit once. Medicine recipient commits with SQ06 consumption and its single ten-crown reward. Ending commits with MQ06 completion, banners, signs and all three checkpoint records; defeated guards and collected loot remain intact. Side quests continue after all three endings. All service state mutations use Transactions; puzzle partial progress and selected tracking are transient.

Completion and choice calls require a nonempty attempt ID. Permanent action identities are service-owned: `quest/<quest_id>/completion`, `choice/wren_terms`, `quest/<quest_id>/activate`, `quest/step/<full_objective_id>`, `read/<document_id>`, and `puzzle/crypt_bell_puzzle/solved`. A different attempt ID cannot re-award a completed quest. Repeating the same committed choice returns its saved receipt; a conflicting choice fails without a receipt or mutation. Reward delivery IDs are `<quest_id>/<item_id>`, producing permanent `reward/<delivery_id>` evidence.

The only non-final conversation steps are the full canonical objective IDs ending in `request_charter`, `meet_wren`, `present_evidence`, `challenge_rusk`, and `discuss_resolution`, with the speakers listed below. A wrong NPC or step fails. Early Elian contact cannot credit her later charter request or attestation. `present_evidence` checks both retained charter and ledger and opens the undercroft. `challenge_rusk` permits a successful replay while Rusk is alive, allowing the runtime boss encounter to restart after recovery; a defeated captain rejects re-challenge. Completing ordinary quests records the final return conversation itself. World/UI callers must first validate the current physical speaker, as the prescribed complete signature carries no NPC argument.

Choice IDs/values: `wren_terms=amnesty|restitution`, `medicine_recipient=camp|village`, and `ending=charter|warden|free_road`. The latter two invoke quest completion internally; direct complete calls refuse them. Wren terms require the active meeting and ledger. Medicine requires the separate camp medicine, never cart medicine. Ending requires active MQ06, Mara's discussion and writ review, and refuses danger, action lock or travel. Reading `village_writ_table` never accepts MQ06. Showing choice information or cancelling confirmation needs no mutation call.

Read-document IDs: `southern_sign`, `toll_notice`, `toll_receipt`, `abbey_inscription`, `orra_charter`, `grain_ledger`, `ferry_ledger`, `captains_order`, `broken_bell_plaque`, `village_writ_table`. The document view returns canonical text for an accessible panel. Journal documents include read flags and acquired documents. Chimes accept only reed, stone and flame. Wrong order resets transient progress without damage; a correct third input commits puzzle_solved. Solved and charter-acquired remain independent.

`journal_view` returns active/completed lists, retained documents and tracked view. Objective rows include label, progress/count, completed, target_id, scene_id, location and optional portal_id. `tracked_view().objective.hint_target_id` points outside to the authored portal for an indoor target. If no quest is active, it points to the next AVAILABLE main giver with `acceptance=true`, without accepting it or revealing unaccepted side quests. Portal IDs are `inn_door`, `crypt_door`, `undercroft_door`; these are physical exterior portals, distinct from the destination scene entrance `entry`.

## Integration and validation

Connect evidence_acquired, inventory_changed and entity_defeated after successful commits to reconcile. Event callbacks that receive an ID can discard it and call reconcile. Reconciliation compares derived quest records first and emits no receipt/event for a no-op, including JSON-loaded numeric counters. It may advance objective counts or unlock quests, but never pays rewards. Root should call reset_runtime on New Game and successful restore, then reconcile after swapping the state. Invalid restore must not reset transient state.

`autosave_requested(reason)` fires after a new committed quest completion or choice, never on replay. T14 must bind it and coalesce unsafe requests. T22 owns the ending save-before-epilogue flow, including Retry save/Continue without saving. This service performs no UI or file I/O and does not claim an autosave succeeded. `epilogue_view` is pure and returns all three panels using the locked ending and current Wren/medicine flags; it never forces presentation on load.

`QuestPredicates.validate_definitions(db) -> PackedStringArray` runs after all registries load. It checks objective IDs, whitelist, counts, fixed pickup item/source matching, distinct candle sources, speakers, portals, rewards and consumption/effects. `QuestPredicates.validate_snapshot(state, db) -> ActionResult` runs after base structural validation. It checks prerequisite order, objective bounds/evidence, completed progress and receipts, reward evidence, consumed-item retention, atomic choices and receipt values, side-effect flags, conversation chronology, Ada access, final review, and resulting checkpoint/banner/sign state. Root must update older tests that forge choice or side-effect flags before enabling strict validation globally.
