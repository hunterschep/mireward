## Behavior delivered

The inn has a common room and sleeping alcove. The crypt has a vestibule, three burial chambers, and a charter vault. The undercroft has a storehouse, a short guard corridor, and a captain's hall. All three have matching floor/wall/furniture collision and connected, obstacle-aware NavigationRegion3D geometry. Stable encounter coordinates remain clear. Original VisualFactory props and shared ArtMesh materials supply furnishings, crypt arches, doors, banners, and lamps.

WorldRouter preserves the single player while constructing destinations in separate SubViewport worlds. It applies detached persistent entity records, checks actual region/map ownership and synchronization, checks grounded capsule clearance and doorway/hazard exclusion, fades, moves the world, waits for the new navigation space again, then commits placement and releases input. Failed preparation leaves the old scene and snapshot intact. If arrival fails after reparenting, the old world is reattached and its runtime references are rebound. Input/cursor policy remains owned by GameModeController.

The exterior has three visible door adapters, including an iron-barred undercroft that refuses access until flags.undercroft_open. The exterior courtyard remains accessible. Doors use the three canonical exterior return IDs. Each interior entry is (0,0,3), with its exit at (0,0,5.5). The inn's bedside anchor remains (-4,0,-2). Rest lantern adapters call the actual RecoveryService.

Nine physical exterior discovery Areas commit each canonical landmark once through Transactions and EventBus. They are connected only in an activated world. Deep water, all exterior bounds, and invalid interior positions recover to a validated local anchor. Invalid anchors use village_shrine and record a diagnostic. Hazard recovery removes ten HP with a one-HP minimum and preserves crowns/progress. Living encounters reset; defeated records and remaining loot survive reconstruction. A future boss world can implement reset_live_encounters() to reset living Rusk and open the gate without setting victory.

Travel acceptance contains pending=true and operation_id. The terminal travel_completed(operation_id,result) signal follows actual arrival/failure and travelling=false. The router calls recovery.finish_pending_travel itself for RecoveryService-owned requests; GameRoot must not duplicate it. Normal scene transitions emit autosave_requested. RecoveryService retains ownership of rest/death autosave signals.

Bind RecoveryService to router.is_dangerous, router.reset_living_encounters, and router.recovery_travel after router.configure. Its apply=false callback is read-only and verifies anchor identity, route, hazards/portals, and local physical safety. Actual placement always performs full destination physics/navigation validation. Snapshot.player.scene_id may already change at a recovery commit, so loaded_scene_id/current_world are authoritative for actual scene lifetime.

T14 prepare_restore validates a detached snapshot and builds a disabled destination in an isolated physics/navigation world. It returns a prepared_token, never live state. commit_restore performs final arrival validation, replaces the snapshot through GameSession.restore, places the player, then emits terminal travel_completed. Invalid saved positions use the authored local safe anchor if valid. discard_prepared frees an unused preparation.

Optional world_builder(world, detached_snapshot) -> ActionResult creates scene-owned persistent entities before state application and navigation checks. It must not mutate GameSession, connect live EventBus/player listeners, discover landmarks, or start audio. Disabled nodes can still receive direct signals. The router restores each node's authored process mode and binds ordinary EnemyActor/EncounterCoordinator player references only after activation. world_activated(world) is the integration signal for remaining live-only wiring. For an enemy factory, configure EnemyActor with its canonical manifest record and coordinator, but defer coordinator.configure(player) until activation. Register entities in world.entities so their apply_persistent_state(record) runs against the candidate snapshot.

## Integration review: complete rollback restoration

Two executed regressions exposed incomplete failed-travel rollback. First, retaining only the top menu mode discarded its parents: failed travel from pause/load/confirmation or dialogue/readable left Back trapped on the travel screen. GameModeController now exposes `snapshot_stack() -> Array[StringName]` and `restore_stack(saved: Array) -> ActionResult`. Restoration validates the entire stack before applying it once, preserving cursor/pause/input ownership and pending recovery safeguards. WorldRouter captures this full stack and restores it after failure.

Second, a destination can pass detached preparation but fail its physical arrival check after joining the live world. Removing the original world runs NpcActor exit-tree cleanup, disabling conversation and removing its EventBus subscriptions. Router rollback previously rebound only ordinary enemies. It now emits `world_activated(old_world)` after restoring current_world/loaded_scene_id and confirming navigation, so the same idempotent GameRoot activation hook reconnects NPC/story adapters. That hook must activate existing content, not construct duplicate content on every notification.

The actual-controller nested-menu regression failed six assertions before the first fix; the real-NPC late-arrival regression failed two before the second. Latest dedicated presentation run: PASS 19 assertions. Interaction/controller suite: PASS 45 assertions, including malformed stack rejection, detached snapshots, full restoration and pending-recovery protection. The final default travel rerun passed all 612 assertions after both fixes, recorded in `/tmp/mireward-t11-review-final.log`. Failure/success logs are `/tmp/mireward-t11-stack-before.log`, `/tmp/mireward-t11-npc-before.log`, `/tmp/mireward-t11-npc-after.log`, and `/tmp/mireward-t11-mode-after.log`.
